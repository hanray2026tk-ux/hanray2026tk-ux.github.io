Why Fanshi

Male beauty is about straight lines, angles and strength.

FAQ

QHow is plastic surgery for men different from surgery for women?
AMen have different bone structure, skin thickness and goals, so plans emphasise contours and overall proportion rather than simply softening features.
QCan men have facial contouring surgery?
AYes. Jaw angle, cheekbone and chin procedures all suit men; the design simply keeps stronger, more angular lines.
QAre scars more visible after plastic surgery in men?
AMale skin is usually thicker with more sebum, so healing can be a little slower, but incisions are placed in concealed locations and are generally inconspicuous with proper care.
QDo men need to stay in hospital for facial contouring?
AMost cases do not require an overnight stay; bone-related procedures may warrant a short observation period, as your surgeon decides.
QHow long is recovery after plastic surgery for men?
AMost people return to daily activities in two or three days, with noticeable swelling easing in one to two weeks and full naturalness taking weeks to months.
