What is surgery-first orthognathic surgery?

  • Surgery on the maxilla and mandible

  • corrects jaw position and function in protrusion, facial asymmetry and similar cases.

  • For patients with unbalanced facial proportions or a long face, the improvement in facial appearance is remarkably clear.

The conventional two-jaw approach requires: preoperative orthodontics → surgery → postoperative orthodontics.
At Fanshi, our new approach is simpler: Surgery → postoperative orthodontics.

The conventional two-jaw approach requires 1 to 1.5 years of preoperative orthodontic treatment, forcing patients through a long, painful and inconvenient period.
With Fanshi's advanced approach, surgery comes first and braces are worn only afterward. This means far less discomfort and a faster path to recovery and results.

Fanshi surgery-first orthognathic surgery shortens the recovery period by 1 year and 6 months, with no preoperative orthodontics.

Surgery-first versus
conventional orthognathic surgery
Difference

People often ask, "How is the surgery-first approach different from orthognathic surgery?" The surgery-first approach is in fact a type of orthognathic surgery; the difference is the order. Conventional orthognathic surgery requires orthodontic treatment first, then surgery. The surgery-first approach skips the long preoperative orthodontics, performing surgery first and orthodontics afterward.

Fanshi: Korea's first
to introduce the surgery-first approach
for orthognathic surgery!

Patients with malocclusion usually want relief as soon as possible. However, many experience noticeable worsening of the jaw during the long preoperative orthodontic period. To reduce this pain and burden, Fanshi was the first in Korea to introduce the surgery-first approach, letting patients have surgery sooner for faster, more comfortable results.

FAQ

QCan international patients use interpretation services?
AYes. Fanshi provides interpretation in your preferred language, with an interpreter beside you through consultation, surgery, follow-up checks and recovery, so you stay comfortable and clearly informed at every step.
QHow long is recovery after surgery?
AIn most cases, mini V-line surgery and cheekbone reduction may allow same-day discharge depending on your condition. The patient's condition must still be confirmed after anesthesia wears off, and the surgical site properly disinfected. Overnight observation is safer. Daily activities can resume 2-3 days after surgery (please read the postoperative manual provided by the clinic). Noticeable swelling and bruising may last 3-5 weeks, and mild swelling 3-6 months. Rinse your mouth frequently with mouthwash and avoid hot or spicy foods, as they can irritate the surgical site. Starting mouth-opening exercises about two weeks after surgery is also recommended to widen the jaw range and help nerves heal faster.
QCan cheekbones grow back after cheekbone reduction?
AFacial bones stop growing once the growth period ends. Cheekbone reduction moves the prominent cheekbone itself inward and fixes it in a new position, so you can be confident that the cheekbones will not become larger again after surgery.
QWill my cheeks sag after cheekbone reduction?
AFirst, precise analysis of the cheekbone shape before surgery is essential, along with consultation with a highly experienced doctor based on X-ray and 3D-CT results. During surgery, the cheekbones are shaved according to the diagnosis. Depending on individual skin elasticity, an additional lifting procedure may be recommended to raise the tissue together for a better lifting effect.
QDo all procedures require an overnight stay?
AIn general, cheekbone reduction patients can be discharged the same day. In many cases, patients operated on in the morning go home alone in the afternoon once anesthesia wears off. However, do not bend your head or look down while traveling home or sleeping. This helps reduce swelling and pain, and using a fluffy pillow to keep yourself upright is recommended. (Please read the postoperative manual provided by the clinic.)
QWill my surgery need fixation pins?
AIn cheekbone reduction, which requires fully cutting the bone, fixation pins hold skin tissue and muscle firmly together so the bone heals faster. The same applies to other facial contouring surgeries. Three months after surgery, the bone is fully healed and as firmly joined as before. The fixation pins are made of surgical and medical metal, the same material used in dental implants, and are harmless to the body; whether to remove them is your choice. They do not affect airport security, CT or MRI examinations.

QHow is T-osteotomy different from conventional osteotomy?
AT-osteotomy cuts the chin bone into a T shape, brings the removed mandibular segments together and fixes them with titanium pins. A square jaw reduction can be performed at the same time for a natural V-shaped face.
