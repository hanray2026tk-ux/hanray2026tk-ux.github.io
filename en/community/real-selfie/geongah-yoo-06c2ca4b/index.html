The beauty I want to show

Breast
GeongAh Yoo
20s
#Motiva#Breast Augmentation#Breast Surgery

< Before >

< After >

At first they felt quite hard, so I was a little worried,

Will they really get softer?

Will they really become springy?

But recently someone who felt them said they were surprised, because there is no foreign body sensation at all.

Hahahahaha

Usually after surgery you can feel something inside, but they told me mine feel really nice to touch ^.^ haha

I think choosing Motiva implants for my breast augmentation was the right call.

I saw some posts saying Motiva feels springy and might therefore look unnatural,

and I wondered whether mine were the same...

But I have no chance to feel anyone else's breasts,

so I had nothing to compare with and could only think that this was probably just how it is ~

But recently when I heard others say there is no foreign body sensation and they feel great,

I felt a little proud, haha

The hotter the weather gets, the more I feel breast augmentation was a really good decision.

Of course, compared with completely natural breasts there may still be a slight difference.

Haha, but I heard that when natural breasts reach around an E cup,

gravity inevitably makes them sag quite a bit,

so the shape may not look as nice.

Actually I have never seen that myself,

and I have never been a natural E cup either, so I do not really know... hahahahaha

Since surgery I keep hearing people compliment my breast shape,

and say my figure is great, so I feel my confidence has gone up a lot.

Now when I pick clothes I find myself leaning toward pieces that show off my figure.

Since I had the surgery I want to show it off properly, haha

I have to make the most of it.

At first, because it was painful and uncomfortable,

I did not understand why people say breast augmentation has the highest satisfaction.

But once the pain and discomfort start to fade,

you understand it all at once.

I am really glad I had the surgery.

I am proud of myself for having the courage to do it, hahahahaha

Honestly I often feel I should have done it sooner,

but I am very happy that I did it now.

From now on I am going to enjoy it even more, haha kiki
