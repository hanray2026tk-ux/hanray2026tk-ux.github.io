Q

I want a high, striking bridge!
Should I choose a thick, tall implant?

A

Asian skin is thicker, but the cartilage and bone that make up the nose are relatively small and thin. It is therefore important not to place an implant that is too high or too large, as over time these can damage your nose and its supporting structures. The alar cartilage is also usually small and fragile, especially when the medial crura of the nasal cartilage are underdeveloped.

The goal of the Fanshi rhinoplasty team is to create a naturally beautiful tip for Asian patients. The appropriate height and tip length are determined by the size and thickness of each patient's cartilage. Drawing on years of experience and research, the Fanshi rhinoplasty team is committed to delivering satisfactory results through precise diagnosis and planning.

FAQ

QCan international patients use interpretation services?
AYes. Fanshi provides interpretation in your preferred language, with an interpreter beside you through consultation, surgery, follow-up checks and recovery, so you stay comfortable and clearly informed at every step.
QWill rhinoplasty make me look as if I had inner canthoplasty?
AEarly in recovery after rhinoplasty, swelling and skin tension make the eyes look closer together, but this returns to its original position within 1 to 2 months. If your eyes sit close together, bridge height should be balanced so the proportions between the eyes stay right.
QIs swelling longer after revision rhinoplasty?
AAlthough revision surgery is more demanding than a first operation, swelling and bruising still settle within about 2 weeks.
QDoes hook nose surgery need an implant?
APeople with a hook nose usually have a high bridge. If a higher bridge is not wanted, surgery can be performed without an implant.
QI had nasal filler before my rhinoplasty.
AAfter nasal filler it is hard to judge the original bridge height. We recommend waiting until the filler has fully absorbed before having rhinoplasty.
QCan rhinoplasty make rhinitis worse?
AMany patients with rhinitis or sinusitis worry that symptoms will worsen. We check the condition with a 3D CT scan before surgery, and this thorough assessment lets us restore nasal function while improving the shape of the nose.
