FAQ

QIs surgery safe? Who will perform my operation?
A

Every operation is performed by a licensed Fanshi physician, supported by a dedicated anesthesiology team and nursing staff. A complete medical evaluation (physical examination plus consultation) precedes surgery, and a structured recovery plan follows it. There is never a 'walk in and operate' approach.

QIs the clinic properly licensed? Can I verify its credentials?
A

Fanshi is a formally licensed medical institution and can provide its Medical Institution Practice License and the license numbers of its operating surgeons for online verification before your visit. We do not rent venues or outsource surgery; all procedures are performed by our own team from start to finish.

QWhat if I am not satisfied with the results or develop a complication?
A

A clear informed consent form and scope of responsibility are signed before surgery. If unexpected issues arise afterward, the original surgical team takes priority in arranging revision. Revision policies and time limits are stated item by item in the contract — never merely promised verbally — so all aftercare commitments are documented.

QHow much will it cost? Are there hidden fees?
A

We useall-inclusive flat-ratepricing, with surgical fees, airport transfers, hotel accommodation and post-operative care itemized separately and fully transparent. A complete cost breakdown is provided before signing, with no hidden charges and no on-site price increases. Final pricing is quoted after assessing your individual procedures and customized plan.

QHow are deposits and refunds handled?
A

The deposit secures your surgery date and reserves hotel and support services. If we cannot perform the surgery as scheduled for reasons on our side, the deposit is refunded in full without conditions. Cancellation and rescheduling rules for personal reasons are written into the contract in advance, so the terms are transparent and documented.

QWill I be lonely coming alone? Is an escort provided?
A

A dedicated companion is provided throughout your stay. Beyond professional medical and post-operative care, we offer local guide services including restaurant recommendations, popular attractions and post-operative leisure activities. You are accompanied at all times and never have to handle medical visits or travel alone, so treatment is easy and recovery is worry-free.

QWhat if I don't speak Chinese or English well?
A

Bilingual interpretation is provided throughout, covering airport pickup, consultation, pre-operative communication, the entire surgery, post-operative checkups and daily travel for zero communication barriers. WhatsApp and WeChat support is available 24 hours a day, with multilingual service in English and Southeast Asian languages to answer your questions at any time.

QHow long do I need to stay in Korea?
A

The length of stay depends on your procedures; a typical stay is 7–14 days. Eye procedures require about 5–7 days before suture removal, nasal procedures about 7 days before splint removal, and more invasive procedures such as liposuction and breast augmentation usually need 10–14 days for basic recovery. We can tailor a combined recovery and holiday itinerary around your surgical plan so you can rest and relax at the same time.

QHow do I follow up after returning home? Who handles any issues?
A

Each client receives a personal recovery file with full post-operative tracking, including scheduled video and photo checkups on days 7, 30 and 90. A dedicated WhatsApp line connects you directly to our aftercare team for 12 months for questions and recovery guidance. If in-person follow-up is needed, we help arrange a return visit or a partner clinic locally, providing comprehensive aftercare support.

QAm I a suitable candidate? Are there age or health restrictions?
A

All clients complete a full pre-operative health screening, including coagulation, cardiopulmonary function and allergy history. Clients who are pregnant or breastfeeding, or who have certain underlying conditions, must postpone surgery or be individually assessed by a physician before proceeding. We adhere to objective professional standards, explain your suitability honestly and never pressure you into procedures.

QHow long do results last? Will they look artificial?
A

We focus onnaturally authenticaesthetic design. For eye, nose and other facial procedures, our core philosophy is to preserve your individual character with refined, natural and harmonious results, avoiding artificial or stiff looks. Duration varies by procedure: double eyelid and rhinoplasty results can remain stable long term, while implant breast augmentation simply requires regular follow-up as advised. Before surgery, your doctor sets complete expectations and clarifies the likely outcome, with no exaggerated claims.

QCan I see real cases? Are the photos genuine?
A

We can providewith written authorization from the patientreal patient recovery cases and live consultation videos. All private information is masked and anonymized to fully protect patient privacy. We never display or misuse cases or materials without formal authorization, and every item is authentic and traceable.
