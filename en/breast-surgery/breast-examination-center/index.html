FAQ

QIs breast reduction suitable for patients who feel physical discomfort from overly large breasts?
A

Suitability for breast reduction is determined through consultation and preoperative medical examination, based on the patient's symptoms and condition.

How suitability is confirmed

  • See the breast reduction page for concerns related to large breasts.
  • Share your symptoms and concerns through online consultation before your visit.
  • When finalizing the surgical plan, your condition is confirmed through a comprehensive preoperative medical examination.

Why consultation matters

  • Fanshi lists the physical issues that may prompt patients to consider breast reduction, including discomfort in the breasts, neck, shoulders and lower back, posture problems, frequent headaches and skin irritation under the breasts.
  • Fanshi explains that before plastic surgery with general anesthesia or a long procedure, heart, lung, liver function and blood pressure should be checked.
  • Online consultation lets patients ask about plastic surgery before visiting.

in Fanshi

  • Fanshi's breast reduction page introduces the procedure and lists details including a surgery time of 2 to 3 hours, general anesthesia and no hospitalization.
  • Fanshi's preoperative examination page describes a diagnostic system involving internal medicine specialists and 50 comprehensive medical tests.
  • Fanshi's online consultation page lets patients ask about plastic surgery, dermatology and dental clinic services.
QIs revision breast surgery more complex than primary surgery?
A

Yes. Fanshi describes breast revision as surgery requiring careful planning, including consultation with breast surgery specialists and cause analysis using breast ultrasound.

When revision surgery should be considered

  • Breast revision may be considered for capsular contracture, breast asymmetry, implant rupture, or dissatisfaction with breast size or shape.
  • Fanshi explains that revision is usually performed 6 months to 1 year after the first surgery, though timing may vary by patient.
  • Patients can ask breast surgery questions through online consultation before visiting.

in Fanshi

  • The breast revision page introduces symptom-specific approaches for capsular contracture, asymmetry, dissatisfaction with size or shape, and implant rupture.
  • The page explains that breast ultrasound is used to analyze the cause before planning revision.
  • The page lists a surgery time of 2 to 3 hours, general anesthesia, no hospitalization, sutures removed about 10 days after surgery and a recovery time of 2 to 4 days.
QDoes a breast lift make breasts bigger, or only lift them?
A

A breast lift mainly corrects sagging, while combining a lift with augmentation can also enlarge the breasts with implants.

How the options compare

  • A breast lift alone uses the patient's own breast tissue to correct sagging, without implants.
  • Fanshi's breast lift page explains that a lift does not change breast size but improves sagging.
  • The lift with augmentation section describes using implants to enlarge the breasts while correcting sagging.

in Fanshi

  • The breast lift page explains that the method depends on the degree of sagging, breast tissue volume and skin elasticity.
  • The Full HD breast augmentation page explains that implant type and size are chosen based on consultation, test results and physical condition.
  • Online consultation lets patients ask breast surgery questions before visiting.
QWhen should I choose a breast lift, augmentation, or both?
A

Patients should compare whether the main concern is sagging, breast size or both, and confirm the right option through consultation.

Differences between the options

  • A breast lift alone is described as surgery that corrects sagging without implants.
  • Lift with augmentation is described as correcting sagging breasts and enlarging them with implants.
  • Full HD breast augmentation selects implant type and size based on consultation, test results and physical condition.

in Fanshi

  • Fanshi explains that the lift technique considers the degree of sagging, breast tissue volume and skin elasticity.
  • The augmentation page describes the process including consultation, implant selection, preoperative design and aftercare.
  • Patients can ask breast surgery questions through online consultation before visiting.
QHow does Fanshi decide the incision for breast augmentation?
A

At Fanshi, the incision plan for breast augmentation is decided after consultation, medical examination and implant selection.

How the surgical plan is made

  • Patients consult a Korean plastic surgery specialist before breast augmentation.
  • Implant type and size are selected based on the consultation, test results and the patient's physical condition.
  • Fanshi explains that the surgical and incision plan is determined after implant selection.
  • The surgical plan is then marked on the patient before surgery.

in Fanshi

  • Fanshi's breast augmentation process includes consultation, implant selection, surgical design and aftercare.
  • Fanshi explains that doctors recommend implants based on factors such as height, weight, chest wall, asymmetry, breast tissue volume and skin elasticity.
  • The breast surgery page explains that the procedure and incision are decided after implant selection.
QHow does recovery differ between a breast lift and implant augmentation?
A

Because the surgical details differ, recovery also differs between breast lift and augmentation.

Recovery information

  • Fanshi lists breast lift with a surgery time of 30 minutes to 1 hour, general anesthesia, no hospitalization, sutures removed about 10 days after surgery, and a recovery time of 2 to 4 days.
  • Fanshi lists Full HD breast augmentation with a surgery time of 1 hour, general anesthesia, no hospitalization, no suture removal, and a recovery time of 3 to 4 days.
  • Postoperative recovery and possible reactions vary by patient.

QHow do the long-term results of a breast lift compare with implants?
A

Breast lift and implants address different issues, so long-term planning should consider sagging correction, volume goals, implant selection and aftercare.

Different goals

  • A breast lift alone is described as correcting sagging without changing size.
  • Lift with augmentation is described as correcting sagging and enlarging the breasts.
  • The Motiva implant page introduces a customized plan based on body and skin type, breast layer analysis and implant selection.

in Fanshi

  • Fanshi describes breast ultrasound before and after Motiva breast surgery.
  • Patients can discuss breast surgery goals through online consultation before visiting.
QHow many days in Korea do breast augmentation, lift or reduction require?
A

Travel timing should be confirmed with Fanshi in advance, because the pages describe examination steps and recovery times but do not give one standard length of stay for all breast surgeries.

How the schedule is arranged

  • Beforehand, patients complete chart preparation, examination consultation and examination date reservation.
  • On the examination day, the process includes reception, changing into a prepared gown, body measurement, X-ray, electrocardiogram, bone density testing and detailed blood tests.
  • Patients can confirm their schedule through online consultation before coming to Korea.

in Fanshi

  • Full HD breast augmentation lists a recovery time of 3 to 4 days.
  • Breast lift lists a recovery time of 2 to 4 days, and breast reduction lists a recovery time of 2 to 4 days.
