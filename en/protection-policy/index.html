Fanshi Privacy Policy

Fanshi (hereinafter referred to as “this Website”) values the privacy of every visitor. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use this Website, submit an online consultation, or upload photos.

Article 1. Types of Data Collected and Methods of Collection

The clinic collects only the minimum information necessary to provide accurate online medical consultation.

  • General personal data: nationality, name (if provided), contact details (e.g., WhatsApp, WeChat, telephone), contact number, Fanshi online consultation, age group, and consultation details.
  • Sensitive health data and images: the desired treatment area/procedure, and uploaded photos used for medical evaluation (frontal, 45-degree, and 90-degree photos).
  • Method of collection: voluntarily submitted by users through the online consultation form on this Website.

Article 2. Purpose of Data Collection and Use

The clinic uses the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Identification of international patient services and assignment by country.
  • Pre-consultation medical assessment: medical staff diagnose and consult based on symptoms and photos.
  • Ensuring open communication channels for responding to inquiries, scheduling appointments, and managing complaints.
  • Sending promotional event information and marketing materials (only with separate explicit consent).

Article 3. Storage and Destruction of Personal Data

  • Retention period: personal data and photos collected for online consultation are retained for 3 years for follow-up management and continuous consultation records.
  • Destruction procedure: when the retention period expires or the user requests deletion, the data is destroyed immediately. Electronic files are permanently deleted using techniques that prevent recovery, and paper documents are shredded.

Article 4. Third-Party Sharing, Entrusted Processing, and International Data Transfers

  • Entrusted processing: to provide seamless international customer service, the clinic entrusts data processing to professional organizations under strict confidentiality agreements:
    • Entrusted organization: Fanshi Customer Service Team
    • Purpose: international client communication management, appointment processing, and authorized marketing communications.
    • Retention: until the entrusted contract expires or the user requests deletion.
  • International data transfers: to provide consultation services, the information you submit may be processed within the authorized scope by our customer service team and necessary technical service providers. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Article 5. User Rights (Global Data Subjects)

Users have the right to access, correct, restrict processing of, or request immediate deletion of their personal data and uploaded photos at any time.

In addition, users have the right to withdraw consent to data processing at any time and the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority (such as the data protection authority in their country) if they believe their data protection rights have been infringed.
