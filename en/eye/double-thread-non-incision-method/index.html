FAQ

QCan international patients use interpretation services?
AYes. Fanshi provides interpretation in your preferred language, with an interpreter beside you through consultation, surgery, follow-up checks and recovery, so you stay comfortable and clearly informed at every step.
QWith non-incisional surgery, will there be stitches to remove?
ANon-incisional surgery creates the double eyelid with sutures. With no incision there is less scarring and swelling, but the sutures are more likely to loosen. Fanshi's Seven-lock non-incisional method makes the most of the technique while overcoming its drawbacks, creating a crease held at seven fixation points that is far less likely to loosen.
QHow long is recovery after surgery?
AThis varies by individual and by method. Some swelling and bruising are usual in the first few days and then settle. Eye makeup can start after 2–3 weeks, and the main swelling may take up to 4 weeks to resolve.
QI worry that incisional surgery will look unnatural.
AExcess fat and muscle can make the eyelid look unnatural, but the Skinny incisional method corrects this to create a slim, natural crease that resembles a non-incisional result.
QWhen can revision double eyelid surgery be performed?
AWe recommend revision surgery six months after the first operation. Revision causes more tissue trauma than a primary procedure, so it requires a highly experienced surgeon.
QI am worried it may come back.
AOuter canthoplasty that cuts only the skin surface has a higher risk of recurrence. We make a full transconjunctival incision, remove the skin that could cause recurrence and fix the eye corner to the periosteum, greatly reducing that risk.
