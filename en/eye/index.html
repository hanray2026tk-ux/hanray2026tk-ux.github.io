Why Fanshi

Natural with eyes closed, expressive with eyes open — the basic standard for a double eyelid.
Form and movement together: we consider the width when eyes are open, and the naturalness and line direction when they are closed.

Tailored to your face: we consider brow-eye distance and face shape to deliver a personalised plan, never a one-size-fits-all template.
Experience and follow-up: grounded in ongoing clinical research and extensive hands-on experience, with follow-up after surgery.

FAQ

QDoes double eyelid surgery hurt?
AThe procedure is done under local anesthesia, so it is essentially painless; mild soreness is possible on the day of surgery.
QWhen can I wear eye makeup after double eyelid surgery?
AEye makeup is usually possible after two to three weeks, once your surgeon confirms healing.
QCan a suture-method double eyelid come undone?
AThe suture method involves smaller wounds and faster recovery, but carries some risk of loosening compared with the incisional method. If it loosens, a revision can be assessed.
QHow wide should a double eyelid be to look natural?
AWidth is decided by your face shape, brow-eye distance and skin thickness; your surgeon gives a more suitable design at consultation.
QCan both eyelids be adjusted together?
AYes. In most cases both sides are treated at once so that width and shape are as symmetrical as possible.
QHow long is recovery after double eyelid surgery?
AMain swelling usually eases within one to two weeks, and the shape settles after about a month.
